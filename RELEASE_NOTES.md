## Walking Pad for Windows

**Download `WalkingPad.exe` below, then double-click it.** It installs itself (no admin rights needed), adds a
desktop icon, starts quietly with Windows so every walk is logged, and opens the app.

**Windows may warn you** ("Windows protected your PC") because the app isn't signed by a paid certificate yet.
Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**You need:** a Windows 10/11 PC with Bluetooth, and a walking pad that speaks the standard Bluetooth treadmill
protocol (FTMS). UREVO pads work out of the box. Turn the pad on; it connects by itself.

**On your phone:** in the app go to Goals → *Use it on your phone* (create your account in Community first).
Apple Health sync is set up from the phone.

**Uninstall:** Start menu → *Uninstall Walking Pad*. Your walking history stays in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WalkingPad`
unless you delete that folder.
